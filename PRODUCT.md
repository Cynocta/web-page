# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, served equally:

- Owners and managers of SMBs (PYMES) in Latin America who lose customers because nobody answers in time and everything still passes through them.
- Operations and sales leads at larger companies (Empresas) who need processes automated and connected to their systems.

They arrive from search, Instagram and referrals, mostly in Spanish (an English tree exists at `/en`).

## Product Purpose

Cynocta is a Colombian software, automation and AI studio. It designs and implements WhatsApp chatbots, AI agents, process automation (n8n), CRM and API integrations, dashboards, custom software and websites, so a business answers 24/7 and stops depending on one person for every task. Success on the site is a visitor booking a diagnosis or opening a WhatsApp conversation.

## Positioning

Expert implementation, in Spanish, for Latin American SMBs and companies: a team that builds and runs the automation on its own infrastructure, not a tool to configure alone.

## Operating Context

- Services (10): WhatsApp chatbot, process automation, AI agents, CRM integration, custom software, API integrations, n8n automations, dashboards, consulting, websites.
- Solutions by segment (5): PYMES, Empresas, commercial automation, operational automation, enterprise AI.
- Own infrastructure on a VPS (n8n, Supabase, Evolution API for WhatsApp).
- Primary contact paths: the diagnosis booking and WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind v4; bilingual es/en.
- Light theme is the default, dark is the alternate; both must work.
- Pages must stay light and responsive: the hero previously carried a 6.6MB WebGL scene and it was removed for that reason.
- Undecided: the hero's primary call to action may change during the current exploration.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Cynocta. Mark: a constructed trinity knot with exact three-fold symmetry, in two weights (refined at 40px and above, compact below). Symbol gradient `#79E2FF → #48EDB8 → #19F59C`; deep variant `#0089AF → #0E9065 → #069059` on light grounds. The wordmark is never a gradient.
- Voice: clear, direct Spanish without hype, aimed at business owners; everyday problems ("nadie le responde a tu cliente a las 11 pm"). No exclamation marks or emoji in content; one highlighted word per headline.
- Value "Decir que no": the studio says no to automating what should not be automated.
- Buttons are square (`--r-sm`, 8px); the pill radius is for chips and labels only.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real products and cases: Taquilla digital por WhatsApp, Kummi, chatbots and agents.
- Stats on the home page (response under 5 s, 24/7, first delivery in 2 weeks) are product targets, not measured customer results.
- No customer testimonials, logos or case metrics with permission yet: never invent them. Every metric needs a source and a date.

## Product Principles

1. Prove with the working system, never with invented numbers.
2. Plain language for owners first; technical depth one level down.
3. Fast and light on any phone: performance is part of the brand promise of automation.
4. The mark carries the identity; the wordmark stays plain.
